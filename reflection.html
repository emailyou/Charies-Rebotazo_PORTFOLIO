My Reflection on Computer Literacy

Making this computer literacy portfolio helped me understand that learning about technology is important for every student. Computers are useful not only for completing school activities but also for communicating, organizing information, and preparing for future employment.

As someone who is still developing my computer skills, I realize that I do not have to know everything immediately. What matters is my willingness to learn, ask questions, and practice the things I find difficult.

I hope to use the knowledge I gain from this subject in my college studies and future career in Financial Management. This portfolio represents my progress as a student and my determination to continue learning. I believe that with patience, hard work, and confidence, I can develop the skills I need to achieve my dreams.
